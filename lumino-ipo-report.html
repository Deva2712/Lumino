Pre-IPO earnings check — mandatory
NO SPIKE OBSERVED — but FY26 profit quality was investigated
EBITDA margin moved gradually (10.31% → 11.62% → 11.71%) with no filing-year jump. However, FY26 PAT grew 28.4% on revenue growth of only 6.4%. The gap came from: other income of ₹48.2 Cr (up from ₹28.7 Cr — 23.6% of pre-tax profit), a lower effective tax rate of 21.9% vs 26.2% (including a ₹4.5 Cr prior-year tax reversal and deferred-tax credits), and FY25's ₹6.4 Cr doubtful-debt provision not repeating. Core operating EBITDA grew just 7.2%. Management attributes the modest revenue growth to lower T&D project execution, offset by EHV substation ramp-up. RHP p.92, 158, 406–407 (MD&A). One-off-assisted, not manufactured — but the operating year was ordinary.
Scenario arithmetic — skipped for this report
The stated intent behind this report is listing-day only. Multi-year scenario extension of historical CAGRs measures nothing relevant to a day-one outcome, and for IPOs the short public track record makes it unusually unreliable anyway — so it is deliberately omitted rather than decorated with numbers.

HIGH
Group entity in the same business
Laser Power & Infra Ltd (listed; ~₹4,200 Cr m-cap) makes cables/conductors and does power EPC from Kolkata — run by Deepak Goel, brother of MD Devendra Goel and a member of Lumino's promoter group. Lumino's TN & Assam divisions were demerged into it via NCLT scheme (RHP p.127–130, 289). Devendra Goel personally guarantees ~₹1,650 Cr of Laser's working-capital lines across 14 banks (p.43–44); reciprocally, Deepak Goel and two family firms guarantee Lumino's facilities. RHP p.316 states promoters are "not involved in any other venture in the same line" — technically true of the three named promoters as directors/shareholders, but the guarantee web and shared history make the two companies financially and competitively entangled. RHP p.445 separately confirms common pursuits exist between group companies and Lumino.

Definitions — tap to expand

RHP — Red Herring Prospectus. The offer document filed with SEBI. Everything the company is legally required to tell you sits in it, including the parts it would rather you skipped.

Fresh issue vs OFS — Fresh issue money goes into the company to fund the business. Offer-for-sale money goes to existing shareholders selling out. A high OFS share means the raise is an exit, not a funding round.

WACA — Weighted Average Cost of Acquisition. What promoters and selling shareholders actually paid for their shares. The gap between WACA and the offer price is the gap between their cost and yours.

Objects of the Offer — The stated uses for fresh-issue money. Specific capex and named debt repayment are checkable. Large "general corporate purposes" is not.

Anchor investors — Institutions allotted shares a day before the issue opens, with staggered lock-ins. Long-only funds signal a different kind of interest than short-horizon money.

Lock-in expiry — Dates when previously restricted shares become sellable. Concentrated expiries create supply.

QIB route — A filing route for companies that do not meet the profitability eligibility test, requiring a much larger institutional allocation. Its presence tells you something about the track record. Lumino filed under the normal profitability route.

CFO ÷ PAT — Cash from operations against reported profit. Persistently below 1 means the profit is not turning into cash.

Contingent liabilities — Obligations that become real only if something happens. Large ones relative to net worth deserve attention.

GMP — Grey market premium. An unofficial, unregulated price quoted before listing. It is sentiment, it is manipulable, and it says nothing about the business.
